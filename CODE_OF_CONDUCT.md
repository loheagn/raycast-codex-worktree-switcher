# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the technical substance of disagreements.
- Respect privacy and never share another person's private data without permission.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `loheagn@icloud.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this policy.
